# Technical Specifications for Distillation Column C-301

## 1. Operating Parameters
- **Maximum Operating Pressure**: 12.0 MPa
- **Normal Operating Pressure**: 11.5 MPa
- **Maximum Temperature**: 360°C
- **Feed Rate**: 12,500 BPSD

## 2. Structural Details
- **Material of Construction**: Carbon Steel ASTM A106 Gr B
- **Main Pipe Size**: 500mm (Schedule 80)
- **Control Valves**: Primary flow control via CV-204
- **Sensors**: Pressure Transmitter PT-301 located at top manifold.

## 3. Maintenance Guidelines
- Ensure secondary containment is active when modifying heat exchanger E-102.
- NDT (Non-Destructive Testing) is required for CV-204 every 6 months to detect flow variance.
